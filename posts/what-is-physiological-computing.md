Every computer you have ever used knows what you type. None of them knows how you are when you type it.

Tired. Focused. Stressed before a big call. Calm after a run. You feel the difference in a second. Your software never does.

## The missing half

AI got very good at words. It writes, plans and answers in a second. But it answers you at 2 a.m., exhausted, the same way it answers you at 10 a.m. after a good night's sleep.

That's the missing half. Words without the state behind them.

## What the word means

Physiological computing means your computer gets the context of your body. Your state becomes something software can use, the way it uses your calendar or your location.

Your body already tells you everything. It whispers. Physiological computing lets you listen inside, and lets the tools you use adapt to you.

## Why now

For 80 years we adapted to computers. Punch cards, keys, mouse, touch, voice. Each time we learned the machine's language.

Now the machine learns ours. That's the turn we are building Mindscriber for.

> Body whispers. Listen inside. Stay ahead.
