The first programmers punched holes in cards. If one hole was wrong, the whole program failed.

Then came the keyboard. Then the mouse. Then touch, and our fingers learned to swipe. Then voice, and we learned to speak slowly and clearly so the assistant would get it.

## Always our job

Look at the pattern. Every new interface made computers easier. And every time, the work of translating was ours. We learned the commands. We learned the gestures. We learned to talk like a robot so the robot would understand us.

## The turn

The next interface flips it. The computer adapts to you.

It starts with something simple. Your state. How you are right now, before you say a word. A tool that knows you are exhausted answers differently. A team that knows it is overloaded plans differently.

## What we are building

At Mindscriber we build for that turn. Scriber O is a small ring on the upper chest. It gives your AI the context of your state, so your tools adapt to you.

80 years of speaking machine. Now the machine learns your body's language, on your terms.
